LilyGO T-Display-S3 · ESP32-S3

DragyDash ESP32

Flash standalone Dragy Pro BLE speed & GNSS telemetry firmware directly to your LilyGO T-Display-S3 from Chrome or Edge — no PlatformIO or Python setup required.

1. Choose Unit Variant & Flash

Requires desktop Chrome, Edge, or Opera over USB-C.

2. Controls

  • Quick Left / Right Tap: Previous / Next display page (remembered across reboots)
  • Long Left Hold: Reset in-memory run stats (peak, distance, averages)
  • Long Right Hold: Disconnect & pause BLE scanning (hold again to resume)
  • Hold Both Buttons: Cycle brightness (DIM / MID / HIGH, remembered across reboots)

Notes & Safety

  • Disconnect the official Dragy app or iOS DragyDash so the Dragy Pro is free to accept the ESP32 BLE connection.
  • Unofficial experimental project — not affiliated with Dragy or LilyGO. Mount securely and never interact while driving.
